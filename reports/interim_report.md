# Interim Report: Ethiopia Financial Inclusion Forecasting
**Date:** February 1, 2026

## 1. Data Enrichment Summary
We enriched the starter dataset with the following:
- **High-Frequency Operator Data:** Added Telebirr and M-Pesa subscriber growth (2021-2024).
- **Infrastructure Metrics:** Included 4G coverage and mobile penetration data from Ethio Telecom and Safaricom.
- **Policy Milestones:** Added the launch of the National Digital ID (Fayda) as a key event impacting KYC ease.

## 2. Exploratory Data Analysis Results
### A. Key Insights
- **Stagnation in Access:** Account ownership growth has decelerated (+3pp in 3 years). The "easy-to-reach" urban market is likely saturated.
- **Mobile Money Dominance:** Mobile money is the primary driver of new usage, even if it doesn't always count as "new" account ownership in Findex surveys.
- **Interoperability Impact:** The integration of EthSwitch has been a bigger catalyst for usage than any single product launch.

### B. Data Quality & Limitations
- **Sparsity:** Findex data points are only available every 3 years, requiring us to rely on proxy indicators (subscriber counts) for 2022, 2023, and 2025.
- **Granularity:** Lack of consistent regional/gender-disaggregated data for the most recent years (2024).

## 3. Preliminary Observations on Event Impacts
- **Telebirr Launch (May 2021):** Shows a massive immediate spike in registration, but a lagged impact (6-9 months) on active usage.
- **M-Pesa Entry (Aug 2023):** Correlates with increased competition and lower transaction costs, driving volume growth in 2024.
